# HHS Medical Note Guide

| **Medical Questions to be included in your Medical Note** **(must be received within 20 days)** |
| --- |
| Please provide relevant medical documentation to support your request. All medical documents are maintained within our secured system of records and will not be released to anyone, except when required by federal law. Medical information required to process your request is limited to documentation on professional letterhead, signed and dated by your medical provider. The medical documentation should provide responses to the following: |
| Question | Explanation |
| 1.What is the nature, severity, and duration of the impairment? (For example, is it a physical and/or mental condition? | For example, is it a physical and/or mental condition? Is it chronic, episodic, or limited in duration? What is the duration of the condition and when/if is recovery expected/not expected? Are the symptoms mild, moderate, or severe? Which body systems and/or organ systems are affected?) |
| 2. What activity or activities (to include major bodily functions) does the impairment limits? | For example, walking, breathing, sitting, circulatory functions, etc. |
| 3.What is the extent to which the impairment limits the individual's ability to perform the activity or activities? | For example, "difficulty sitting for more than 30 minutes causing…"; using computer monitor for more than 1-hour exacerbates…" |
| **Explanation for use when completing questions 4 and 5:** What is the nexus between the medical condition and the medical provider’s recommended accommodation? (For example, someone with a vision-related disability may benefit from a monitor magnifier but would most likely not use a scooter.) (Also, what is the benefit of the recommended accommodation to the requestor? For example, does it lessen pain, prevent exacerbation of symptoms, enables to perform certain duties?) For example, what is the trigger (event, area, etc.) creating the barrier for the requestor? Please define and detail as specifically as possible. |
| 4. Explain why the individual requires a reasonable accommodation **OR** |
| 5.Explain why the individual requires a **particular or specific** reasonable accommodation. |
